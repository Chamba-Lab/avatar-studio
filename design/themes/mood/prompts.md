## rakim-rage

Attach `home/brand/discord/avatar-christmas-1024.png` (style) and a frame of the angry-coffee GIF
(pose and expression).

```
Generate 4 different variations (separate images).
Create a single sticker illustration of the attached character (a fluffy black-and-white raccoon-like mascot called Rakim) in a manga RAGE moment: head and shoulders only, mouth wide open shouting with small sharp fangs and a pink-red mouth, furious slanted brows, eyes with a red glow, a red anger vein mark (#E53935) on his forehead, fur bristling outward. Same face markings and fur shapes as the reference.
Style: bold uniform black outlines with rounded ends, flat colours with one soft shade, cream highlights (#FCF6E8), red accents (#E53935, #B71C1C), ink #111111. Cute and cheeky, not scary. No text, no letters, no gradients, no 3D, no drop shadow, no white border.
Composition: centred, filling about 80% of a square 1024x1024 canvas.
Background: fully transparent. If transparency is not possible, a solid flat magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it.
```

The screentone frames are generated by `frames.cjs`: run `node design/themes/mood/frames.cjs <output folder>` from the repo root and copy the SVGs into `src/themes/mood/frames/`.

## on-fire

Generated with ChatGPT, then fitted into a 1024 × 1024 WebP: the fire scaled to 900 px wide, its
bottom on the bottom edge, and everything outside the circle cut away.

```
Generate 4 different variations (separate images).
A circular avatar frame decoration: cartoon fire ONLY along the bottom half of a circle, like a crescent of flames hugging the lower edge of a round profile picture. 6 to 8 tall, wavy, flowing flame tongues of different heights, tallest in the middle, getting smaller toward the sides, with curling tips. The upper half of the circle is completely empty.
Style: flat vector cartoon fire in the spirit of the "This is fine" meme, bold uniform black outline (#111111) around the whole fire shape, flat colours: outer flames red-orange #FF6A1A, inner flames yellow #FFC531, a small hot core #FFF3B0. One soft shade at most. No gradients, no glow, no sparks, no embers, no smoke, no text.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas. The fire follows the bottom edge of an imaginary circle that fills the whole canvas; the centre of the canvas stays empty and transparent so a face shows through. Flames reach at most one third of the way up.
Background: fully transparent. If transparency is not possible, a solid flat magenta #FF00FF with nothing else on it.
Avoid: triangles or spiky zigzags, repeated identical flames, flames around the whole circle, flames pointing to the centre.
```
